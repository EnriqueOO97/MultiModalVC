The special aspect of this architecture is the training during phase 1, where the discriminator is not active, during phase 1 of training the objective is to focus on speech reconstruction, so there is a total loss that is composed of 2 terms, the mel loss and the conversion loss, the conversion task has the characteristic that it is computed from latent features. Essentially the forward pass of the model during phase 1 is divided in 2 internal steps, the first step is so-called canonical step which goes all the way to end to compute the mel loss, the second step is the synthetic step for the conversion loss, this is how it is computed, it uses the latent features produced during the canonical step, these I call them teacher latent features, the teacher latent features come from the conformer, and the conformer always receives the speaker embeddings of reference speaker.

Let me describe the canonical step, during this process the model receives canonical data as input, video and audio, there is fusion, then there is also a speech rate predictor that depending on the speech rate of the input, determines a certain amount of queries, then a Q former module as well that receives audio-visual tokens, then there is the conformer, the conformer always receives speaker embeddings via cross attention, during the canonical step the model receives the speaker embeddings of the canonical audio, and then the output of the conformer (the latent features) are saved for later, but it is also given to the vocoder to produce the waveform, from these waveform the mel spectrogram is produced, and for supervision the groundtruth is also canonical data, therefore this means that the model is not doing voice conversion at this point, it is being focused on speech synthesis, in an autoencoder fashion.

Then in the synthetic step the model receieves a canonical video paired with a synthetic audio, (the synthetic audios are voice converted versions of the canonical audios), then the data goes through the model up until the conformer and produces the the synthetic latent features, the conformer also receives the speaker embeddings of the canonical data, this is why it is called conversion loss, the model receives a synthetic audio and the speaker embeddings as the reference and the conformer should output features that look like the canonical features, then the conversion loss can finally be computed using the teacher latent features as supervision, then the conversion loss is computed so that the synthetic latent features are pushed towards the canonical latent features space/distribution. during the synthetic step, notice that the decoder is not receiving these synthetic latent features, therefore, the synthetic pass behaves as passive learning while the main focus during phase 1 is speech reconstruction from canonical to canonical.

Now, during the training I trigger evaluations after each epoch, since this is speech I decided to use 2 metrics as a sort of reference for the performance and capabilities because at least for speech there is no definitive way to measure how good the output speech is, there are subjective metrics that measure quality, others that measure word or character error rates etc but in this case I check the ssim and the mcd, the structure similarity index which was designed for images and the mel cepstral distortion to measure the spectral difference between 2 audio signals.

In the case of the ssim, it essentially treats the mel spectrograms that are generated from the waveforms as images, therefore, ideally, the predicted mel spectrogram and the ground truth mel spectrogram should look very similar and the ssim should be closer to 1, it goes from 0 to 1

The mel cepstral distortion is in dB and as the training progresses, the mcd decreases, then if you pay attention, as the training progresses you might noticed that as the ssim increases (it goes from 0 to 1) the mcd decreases, from over 100dB to lower dBs

Then, more about the model, as I mentioned the focus of the model during phase 1 is to do speech synthesis/reconstruction, when I see that the model has converged during phase 1 (this I determine it with the total loss but also looking at the validation metrics like the ssim and mcd which give me an idea of the model's behavior) then I actiavte the discriminator for the adversarial training, and I also change the training strategy, during the adversarial training the focus is now voice conversion, so the model doesnt have anymore a forward pass composed of 2 internal steps, during this phase 2 the model has a simple straightforward pass where the input data is audio visual of course, but this time the audio must be synthetic audio, the conformer receives the speaker embeddings of the canonical speaker, and the ground truth for supervision is of course the canonical audio, thus, the model learns to do voice converting by reversing the synthetic audio inputs and convert them into the canonical audios. During phase 2 the conformer module and all the modules prior to the conformer are frozen, so only the vocoder and the discriminator are trainable.
